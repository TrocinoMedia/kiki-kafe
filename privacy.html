Privacy Policy

Effective July 2026

Kiki Kafe ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website at kikikafe.net. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information. By using our site or submitting any form, you agree to the practices described here.

Information We Collect

We only collect information you voluntarily provide through our forms. Depending on which form you submit, this may include:

  • Full name, email address, and phone number
  • Employment history, availability, and resume (job applications)
  • Event details such as date, venue, and guest count (Kiki Kart bookings)
  • Business background and financial information (franchise inquiries)
  • Any additional details you include in open text fields

We do not use cookies, tracking pixels, or analytics tools, and we do not automatically collect device or browsing data.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you submit solely to respond to your inquiry or process your request — specifically:

  • To follow up on job applications and schedule interviews
  • To provide availability and pricing for Kiki Kart event bookings
  • To respond to franchise inquiries
  • To answer any general questions you send us

We do not use your information for marketing purposes without your explicit consent.

How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Form submissions on this site are processed by FormSubmit.co, a third-party form delivery service that transmits your submission to our team via email. FormSubmit's own privacy practices apply to data in transit through their service.

We may share your information if required by law or to protect our legal rights.

Data Retention

We retain submitted information only as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purpose for which it was provided — typically the duration of our correspondence or hiring process. You may request deletion of your information at any time (see Your Rights below).

Your Rights

Depending on where you are located, you may have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Opt out of any future communications from us

To exercise any of these rights, email us at mgmt@kikikafe.net with your request. We will respond within 30 days.

Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has submitted information through our site, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

External Links

Our site contains links to third-party services including our online ordering platform and social media profiles. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to review their policies independently.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of our site after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
